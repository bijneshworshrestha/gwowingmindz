A Note for Grown-Ups

Every book closes with a page for you.

In The Broken Wheel, Zeno wants to fix Lemi's cart for her. Mira pauses and asks a question instead. The note at the end explains what that pause gives a child.

The feeling of I did this myself. That feeling is where confidence actually comes from. From the note in The Broken Wheel

With Written Cultural Heritage of Nepal (WCHN)

Tales of the Heritage Trekkers

A children's book series that passes Nepal's manuscript traditions on to the next generation through storytelling. The stories do not follow real artefacts, but each one shows a real situation: how a manuscript ends up neglected, and what it takes to find, save and protect it.

By Bidur Bhattarai and Bijneshwor Shrestha

See the series at WCHN

Kept accurate by a manuscript researcher

About

A studio for growing minds.

Friends who explore life and the world around them gently. They help each other grow without judgement, and every child can call them a companion. Everyone has a part to play, and that is a strength of its own.

Gwowing Mindz was founded by Bijneshwor Shrestha, a storyteller and illustrator who creates characters and stories rooted in South Asian culture, with a particular focus on Nepal. He designs characters that children can recognise as their own.

The books are written in English. Nepali and Newari editions are being planned.
