About

A studio for growing minds.

Gwowing Mindz is a children's story studio. We create characters, and tell the stories that bring them to life, so children can get to know the places and cultures of the world through friends they recognise. Our first stories are set in Nepal.

Friends who explore life and the world around them gently. They help each other grow without judgement, and every child can call them a companion. Everyone has a part to play, and that is a strength of its own.

Gwowing Mindz was founded by Bijneshwor Shrestha, a storyteller and illustrator who creates characters and stories rooted in South Asian culture, with a particular focus on Nepal. He designs characters that children can recognise as their own.

The books are written in English. Nepali and Newari editions are being planned.
