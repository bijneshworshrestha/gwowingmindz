A Note for Grown-Ups

Every book closes with a page for you.

In The Broken Wheel, Zeno wants to fix Lemi's cart for her. Mira pauses and asks a question instead. The note at the end explains what that pause gives a child.

The feeling of I did this myself. That feeling is where confidence actually comes from. From the note in The Broken Wheel

Questions from the books to ask as you read

  • Which one is most like you? Which one is most like me?
  • How do you feel when a day doesn't go the way you planned?
  • What's something small you could let your child try to fix themselves this week?
