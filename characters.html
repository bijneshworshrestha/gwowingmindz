Characters

Ten characters so far, in two series.

Remi

Son, toddler

Wants to eat the crackers early. Dreams about the next puddle.

SPLASH!

Tenzing

The Guide · Yak

Knows the forgotten trails. Likes the idea of warm Mo:Mo: and butter tea.

I left it just as I found it.

Mira

Mother

Packs the sandwiches. Sips her tea while the rain falls.

What if we let her try first?

Sunauli

The Connector · Danphe

Knows who to call. Pops on a tiny umbrella hat when it rains.

Don't untie it. And don't move it.

Lemi

Daughter, age 5

Picks the perfect picnic spot. Fixes her own red cart.

The sky is LEAKING!

Ganesh

The Teacher · Hatti

Reads the old language. Puts his gloves on first, and brings a raincoat for a wet friend.

Here, Tenzing! You're soaking wet.

Habre

The Artisan · Habre

Knows how old paper is made. Carries a manuscript in both paws, slowly and carefully.

I will keep the manuscript safe.

Zeno

Father

Carries the picnic basket. Goes to bed with mud still in his ear.

The blanket! The sandwiches!

Mitini

The Researcher · Gainda

Asks the questions and keeps the notes. Arrives with her magnifier and soft brushes.

I will take notes.

Dolma

Tenzing's wife · Yak

Opens her home to the friends when they come in from the rain, with hot butter tea.

Namaste! Welcome to our home.
